# JOHNNY BLOCK — BLOCKCHAIN HEIGHTS
## Collection Lore & Rarity Bible — Draft 01

### The World

Blockchain Heights was not built by old money.

It rose block by block from abandoned streets, late-night ideas, coded deals, neighborhood hustle, and people who understood that ownership was changing before the rest of the city did.

At the center of it all is **Johnny Block**.

Johnny did not inherit the Heights. He learned its streets when the lights were still broken, watched digital fortunes appear and disappear overnight, and built his name by knowing one rule: **every block has value if you know what to build on it.**

As Blockchain Heights grew, the city divided into three districts. Every Johnny Block NFT represents a moment, reputation, fit, possession, location, or chapter from that climb.

---

## 💚 STREET RARE — THE BLOCKS
**IDs #0001–#0700 · 700 supply · 70%**

This is where the story begins.

The Blocks are the living heart of Blockchain Heights: neon corner stores, murals, rooftops, late-night streets, underground markets, train platforms, basketball courts, garages, and alleys glowing green from the network running beneath the city.

Street Rare Johnny isn't poor Johnny. He's **building Johnny**.

These pieces capture the years when reputation mattered more than status. Some traits are everyday pieces. Others hint that Johnny is already moving differently from everyone around him.

### Street Background Lore

**Blockchain Heights Street** — The main strip. Every deal, rumor, rivalry, and opportunity eventually passes through here.

**Green Neon Alley** — Hidden behind the storefronts is a narrow alley illuminated by the green glow of the chain. People who know the Heights know this is where private business gets handled.

**City Rooftop** — Johnny's thinking spot. From here he can see the Upper District he intends to reach.

**Block Corner** — Where Johnny first became known. Nothing flashy was required; showing up consistently built the reputation.

**Crypto Storefront** — One of the first businesses in the Heights to accept the city's digital currency. Johnny was there before the crowds.

**Midnight Court** — The neighborhood court after dark. Games here settle bragging rights, business arguments, and sometimes who gets invited into the next move.

**Garage 21** — Part garage, part workshop, part unofficial headquarters. Several of Johnny's earliest ideas were built here.

## 💎 ELITE RARE — THE UPPER DISTRICT
**IDs #0701–#0950 · 250 supply · 25%**

Success changes the view.

Above the Blocks sits the **Upper District**, where Blockchain Heights becomes brighter, faster, and much more expensive. The streets turn electric blue and purple. Rooftops become penthouses. Cars become machines nobody downstairs has seen yet. Invitations matter.

Elite Rare represents Johnny after the city knows his name.

But entry into the Upper District is not simply purchased. It is earned through combinations of rare fits, jewelry, technology, accessories, locations, and status symbols that tell pieces of Johnny's rise.

### Elite Background Lore

**Upper District Skyline** — The towers built from the wealth generated below. Johnny can finally look down at the rooftop where he once planned his future.

**Electric Penthouse** — Glass walls, blue city light, and a private view of the entire chain.

**Validator Lounge** — An invitation-only room where some of the Heights' biggest decisions are made.

**Neon Exchange** — The financial center of Blockchain Heights. Numbers never stop moving here.

**Skybridge 88** — A suspended bridge connecting the two most powerful towers in the Upper District. Access is restricted.

**Private Terminal** — Success has changed how Johnny moves. No lines. No crowds. No explanations.

---

## 👑 GENESIS LEGENDARY — THE GOLDEN REALM
**IDs #0951–#1000 · 50 supply · 5%**

Most residents of Blockchain Heights debate whether the Golden Realm is even real.

It is.

Hidden above the visible city is a district that does not appear on ordinary maps. Black architecture, liquid-gold light, private vaults, ancient blocks, and artifacts connected to the earliest days of the network.

Only fifty Johnny Blocks belong here.

Genesis Legendary pieces should not simply look more expensive. They reveal parts of the story that the Street and Elite pieces cannot.

### Genesis Background Lore

**Golden Realm** — The hidden level above Blockchain Heights. Only Genesis holders have seen the entire city from here.

**Genesis Vault** — A black-and-gold chamber containing the first block and artifacts from the city's creation.

**Throne of Blocks** — Not a royal throne in the traditional sense. It represents control earned through ownership, knowledge, and survival.

**Block Zero Chamber** — The oldest room in the Heights. Its walls contain the original network markings.

**Golden Skyline** — Blockchain Heights after the entire network synchronizes at midnight. For a few seconds, every building turns gold.

# The Hidden Story

Across the 1,000 pieces, traits can act as clues.

A pendant may identify a district. A hand item may reference a deal from Garage 21. Certain eyewear may reveal hidden symbols in a background. A Genesis artifact may connect directly to something visible—but unexplained—in a Street Rare piece.

This allows the collection to have **visual lore**, not just rarity percentages.

Collectors should be able to discover connections such as:

- a Street object later appearing as a priceless Genesis artifact;
- the same skyline seen from progressively higher locations;
- jewelry evolving as Johnny moves through the districts;
- recurring numbers, symbols, businesses, and locations;
- extremely rare trait combinations that reveal a complete story when viewed together.

# Core Collection Rule

**Street tells you where Johnny came from.  
Elite shows you what Johnny built.  
Genesis reveals what Johnny discovered.**

The rarity system should support that story rather than simply making random traits expensive.
