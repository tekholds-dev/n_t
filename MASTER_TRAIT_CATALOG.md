Street favors green/black, silver/diamond, distressed luxury and city-building lore.
Elite favors blue/purple, fur/designer materials, advanced technology, travel and access.
Genesis favors black/gold, founder artifacts, lions/crowns/keys and Golden Realm lore.

## Build rule
A trait can be visually related across tiers but must have a tier-specific production PNG. Never let the generator select a Genesis asset from a Street folder.
