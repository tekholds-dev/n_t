# Johnny Block — Production Art Specification

The approved visual target is the high-detail **Johnny Block / Blockchain Heights** character direction: a confident stylized Black male lead with an exaggerated swept blond hairstyle, dark eyewear, jewelry, luxury streetwear and a futuristic blockchain-city world. Johnny Block must remain an original character rather than a reproduction of an existing television character.

## Locked collection structure
- Street Rare: #0001–#0700 — green / black / neon Blockchain Heights
- Elite Rare: #0701–#0950 — electric blue / purple Upper District
- Genesis Legendary: #0951–#1000 — black / gold Golden Realm

## Production layer stack
Background → Body → Outfit → Hair → Eyewear → Chain → Hand → Special → Effects

Each tier has its own nine folders. This prevents a Genesis-only asset from accidentally appearing on a Street NFT and makes the three visual levels intentional.

## Master art rules
All production PNGs are 2048×2048 with transparency where appropriate. Every interchangeable asset must use the same canvas, character pose, head position, body silhouette, crop and anchor points. Do not flatten wearable traits into the Body master. Keep hair, eyewear, outfit, chain, hand props, specials and effects independently composable.

## Visual progression
**Street:** energetic green neon, distressed luxury streetwear, silver/diamond chains, city/block imagery.  
**Elite:** cleaner premium materials, electric blue/purple light, richer jewelry, Upper District technology and access.  
**Genesis:** restrained black/gold palette, unique artifacts, founder symbolism, Golden Realm environments and unmistakable legendary effects.

## Quality gate
No final 1,000-piece generation until the production PNG layer pack replaces the geometric test assets, layer alignment is visually approved, tier-exclusive traits are verified, and uniqueness capacity exceeds 1,000 combinations.
